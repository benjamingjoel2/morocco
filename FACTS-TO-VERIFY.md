# Facts to verify before launch

Everything below appears on the site and was written from general knowledge (October 2026). Entry rules and prices change, so please confirm each item against an **official source** before publishing. Suggested sources are listed for each section.

Where each fact appears: **TT** = Travel Tips page, **D** = Destinations page, **TD** = Things to Do page, **H** = Home page.

---

## 1. Visas & entry (TT → "Visas & entry")

| # | Claim on the site | Check with |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Citizens of the UK, EU member states, USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand can visit Morocco **visa-free for up to 90 days** as tourists. | Moroccan Ministry of Foreign Affairs (consulat.ma), your government's travel advice (e.g. gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/morocco, travel.state.gov) |
| 1.2 | Some other nationalities need a visa or can apply for an **e-visa** through Morocco's official portal. | acces-maroc.ma (official e-visa portal) |
| 1.3 | The passport must be valid for the whole stay, and **at least six months' validity** is widely recommended. | Government travel advice and your airline |
| 1.4 | You may be asked for an **onward/return ticket** and your **accommodation address**. | Government travel advice |
| 1.5 | **Drones** need prior authorisation and are routinely held at customs. | Moroccan customs (douane.gov.ma) and government travel advice |
| 1.6 | No special vaccination is listed as an entry requirement. (The site doesn't claim this, but travellers will ask.) | WHO / national travel-health service (e.g. travelhealthpro.org.uk, CDC) |

## 2. Currency & money (TT → "Money")

| # | Claim on the site | Check with |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | The currency is the **Moroccan dirham (MAD)**. | Bank Al-Maghrib (bkam.ma) |
| 2.2 | The dirham is a **closed currency**: only a **limited amount** may be taken in or out. *The site deliberately gives no figure. If you want one, verify the current import/export limit (often quoted as 2,000 MAD).* | Office des Changes (oc.gov.ma), Moroccan customs |
| 2.3 | Keep **exchange receipts** to change dirhams back when you leave. | Office des Changes |
| 2.4 | ATMs are common in cities and towns but **scarce in the desert and mountains**, and riads and larger hotels usually take cards. | General knowledge, reasonable but worth a sense-check |
| 2.5 | Tipping guidance: round up in cafés, **~10% in restaurants**, tip guides, drivers and riad staff. | Local partners |
| 2.6 | Contact form budget bands are in **euros**. Decide whether you want EUR, USD, GBP or MAD. | Your business decision |

| 3.8 | Ramadan moves **about 11 days earlier** each year. For 2027, Ramadan is expected around **early-to-mid February**. *The site doesn't give dates.* | Ministry of Habous / moon sighting |
